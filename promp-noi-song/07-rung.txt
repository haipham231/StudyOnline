A single wide landscape illustration of one place, for a Vietnamese preschool app where a child drags a cartoon animal sticker into the scene.

The place: A cool green forest: tall pine and oak trees, a soft mossy floor with little red mushrooms and fallen leaves, gentle dappled sunlight between the trunks.

Very important:
- NO animals of any kind in the picture. No birds, no fish, no insects, no pets, no people. The scene must be completely empty of living creatures, because the child will place the animal themselves.
- Leave a clear open space in the centre foreground, where an animal sticker will be placed.
- Keep everything important in the middle band of the picture. The top and bottom edges will be cropped away, so do not put anything important there.

Style: soft hand-painted children's picture-book background, gentle watercolour and gouache look, bright and friendly colours, slightly simplified shapes, instantly recognisable even for a three-year-old. Not 3D, not photo-realistic. Calm and clear, not busy.

Rules: NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO signs with writing, NO watermark, NO border or frame around the picture.
